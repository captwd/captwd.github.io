Local asset `gymnopedie-no1.mp3` was transcoded from the original Ogg FLAC to 128 kbps MP3. The performance was not rearranged or shortened. `gymnopedie-no1.peaks.json` contains amplitude envelopes derived from that MP3 for the moving visual background.

`gymnopedie-no1.terrain.json` contains a quantized short-time spectrum derived from the same MP3, analyzed at 16 kHz with a 2048-sample Hann window, approximately 10 frames per second and 64 logarithmic frequency bands. It drives the three-dimensional sound terrain.

The site also retains the user's existing Matcha-TTS samples; this credit concerns the background piano recording only.
